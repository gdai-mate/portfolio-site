Story

I've been the one with a plate in each hand.

In September 2022 I moved over to Straddie and started working at a cafe that was losing money. Seven grand a week, on a good week. Then the whole crew quit, right as I got there.

The owner asked what we do now. There was a liquor licence sitting there doing nothing, and I knew how to make a margarita, so I pitched a Mexican restaurant. We found a chef and rebuilt it from scratch. When I left in March 2024 it had done $2.2M in the previous twelve months, six times what the cafe was turning over, and it's still running above that today.

Hospo is bloody hard, the messiest industry going with the thinnest margins. I wouldn't recommend owning one to anyone, ha.

Seventy-hour weeks got it open. Then I got obsessed with the boring stuff, tighter systems and properly backing the people running them. I'd watch how each person actually did their job and build the system around that. Most of the time the fix was putting a bit more weight behind what was already working.

When AI went mainstream in 2022 I saw a gaping hole. Hours and money going into systems that were already old the day AI landed, and the nights owners and their staff lose to admin when they should be home. So I taught myself to use it, then to build it. Two years of sixteen-hour days and no degree, because I couldn't leave it alone.

All those years as a generalist turned out to be my edge. I've poured beers, ripped out kitchens, built gardens and run a restaurant with thirty-five staff, so I know how a business runs from the floor up. The systems I learned there and AI work like a lever and a fulcrum, and depending on the job either one can play either part. Set them up right and the boring work and the bottlenecks start shifting on their own, so more cash comes through the door. And anything I'd put in your business, I've run in mine first.
