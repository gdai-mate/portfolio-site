## 2. THE PROBLEM  (SB7: villain + external/internal/philosophical | Suby 5)

Kicker: 01 · Sound familiar

H2: You started this to be your own boss.

Somewhere along the way the business started bossing you.

Quotes get written on the couch at nine. Invoices stack up until Sunday arvo. The phone rings while you're halfway up a ladder, and that job goes to whoever picked up first. Your best worker asks the same question for the third time this month, because the answer only lives in your head.

That's what happens when every system in a business lives with the owner. Everything runs through you, so everything waits on you.

And then there's the hum in the background. Lying in bed doing maths on a job you quoted three weeks ago.

> You should get to be great at your trade and still have a life outside it.
